## 10:41 · Recursive improvement

**[10:41]** What is recursive self-improvement?

**[10:43]** Recursive self-improvement is using AI agents to do AI research. That looks like closing the loop on both both AI software design and AI hardware design. The idea of recursive self-improvement goes back a long way. In 1965, I Good wrote about the possibility of what he called an intelligence explosion. He said that an ultra intelligent machine might be the last invention that humanity need ever make. And the reason is that as we develop these AI systems and train AI systems that can do AI research, we close the loop and get to a point where AI builds better AI.

**[11:21]** Today, some of the world's most brilliant people are being paid very high salaries to do AI research at the frontier labs. At some point they will effectively automate themselves and this is an act area of active research and even today we know that frontier model training is largely aided with the help of existing AI systems. So recursive self-improvement is when we close the loop on AI research and it happens faster and faster overnight. I think it's worth thinking about recursive self-improvement as the moment where the development of AI technology is no longer in the hands of humans but primarily in the hands of AI itself.

**[12:08]** And from there we're no longer the most intelligent beings and we increasingly hand over the future to our successors. This is a significant moment and it's it's either coming very soon has has already already arguably started.
